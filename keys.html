Free AI keys that work with Verba

Any one of these unlocks the AI answer checking and the “Ask the AI” box. All have a free option without a credit card. Paste the key on the start screen; the site reads the key’s first letters to tell which service it belongs to.

Groq Best first choice

Get a key
console.groq.com/keys (free account, no card)
Free limits
About 30 requests a minute and roughly 1,000 a day on the Llama 3.3 70B model the site uses.
Key starts with
gsk_
Notes
Very fast. This is the one the site has been using all along.

Google Gemini Good backup

Get a key
aistudio.google.com/apikey (Google account)
Free limits
Free on the Flash and Flash-Lite models, which is what the site uses. Google changes the exact numbers often and shows your current ones in AI Studio. The Pro models are no longer free.
Key starts with
AIza
Notes
On the free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products, and people may review it.

OpenRouter

Get a key
openrouter.ai/keys (free account, no card)
Free limits
Models whose names end in :free cost nothing: 20 requests a minute and 50 a day (1,000 a day if the account has ever bought $10 of credit). The site only picks free models.
Key starts with
sk-or-
Notes
One key, many models. Free models can be busy at peak times and get swapped out.

Cerebras

Get a key
cloud.cerebras.ai
Free limits
Free trial tier: 5 requests a minute and about 1 million tokens a day.
Key starts with
csk-
Notes
Extremely fast, but 5 a minute is tight if you ask a lot of questions. Some recent reports say new sign-ups now get trial credit and may need a card, so check before relying on it.

Mistral

Get a key
console.mistral.ai/api-keys (free “Experiment” plan)
Free limits
About 1 request a second, with a large monthly token allowance.
Key starts with
No special prefix, so choose “Mistral” in the AI provider list.
Notes
Needs phone verification, and the free plan requires agreeing that Mistral may train on your requests.

Hugging Face

Get a key
huggingface.co/settings/tokens (free account; make a token with “Make calls to Inference Providers” allowed)
Free limits
A small monthly credit (about $0.10) shared across many hosted models. Enough for a modest amount of practice each month, then it stops until next month.
Key starts with
hf_
Notes
Huge model catalog, but the smallest free allowance here. Good as a spare.

Cohere

Get a key
dashboard.cohere.com/api-keys (a free trial key is made when you sign up, no card)
Free limits
1,000 requests a month and 20 a minute, for non-commercial use.
Key starts with
No special prefix, so choose “Cohere” in the AI provider list.
Notes
Uses Cohere’s Command A model. Fine for personal studying.

Checked, but they don’t work here

NVIDIA NIM
Generous free tier (about 40 requests a minute), but NVIDIA’s servers refuse requests that come straight from a web page, which is how this site talks to the AI.
Cloudflare Workers AI
Needs your account ID as well as a token, and its API is meant to be called from a server, not a web page.
BazaarLink
A small reseller that passes your requests on to other companies. Its free limits aren’t clearly published and there’s little independent information about it, so it isn’t listed.

Free limits change often; these were checked in October 2026. Your key stays in the open tab only and is never saved by the site. Treat a key like a password: don’t share it or post it anywhere.
